# AUTOMO.ID — CUSTOMER LANDING PAGE

Build the public-facing **Customer Landing Page** for Automo.id.

The primary objective is to convert visitors into customers who submit a new car purchase request.

## Primary Conversion Goal

The main CTA must be:

**"Cari Diskon Terbaik"**

Secondary CTA:

**"Request Mobil"**

The user should immediately understand:

> "Saya cukup memasukkan mobil yang saya inginkan, lalu sales akan bersaing memberikan penawaran terbaik."
